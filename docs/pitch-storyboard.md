# Lotline pitch film

Delivered: 54 seconds, 16:9, 1920 × 1080, with the user-selected Ainsley narration and 13 speech-aligned captions. Exact generation and timing records are in [pitch-narration.json](video/pitch-narration.json), and confirmed output URLs are in [delivery.json](video/delivery.json). Example fixtures are not presented as live prices.

## Creative direction

Warm ivory, forest green, quiet precision. The film combines native Higgsedit motion graphics with actual Lotline UI screenshots and the original three-bar SVG mark. Typography and exact allocations are authored graphics; no generated footage or simulated click recording is presented as an observed transaction.

Avoid green-screen presenters, stock market footage, luxury lifestyle imagery, price charts, token rockets, testimonials, claims of returns, and fake purchase confirmations. Do not represent any generated person as a real customer.

The final narration contains 84 whitespace-delimited words across six naturally paced takes. Speech was checked against the authored text using Whisper. The original 54-second edit was preserved without acceleration or time-stretching; only trailing silence in take five was trimmed.

## Higgsfield opener prompt

The delivered opener uses original native Higgsedit shapes. The early generation prompt below was not used in the finished film.

Create a premium minimal product-brand opener on a warm ivory paper background, hexadecimal F7F7F2. Three simple forest-green vertical bars, each equally narrow, aligned to one thin horizontal baseline, heights increasing left to right, settle gently into place with refined easing. Forest green 174D3C and charcoal 18211D. Soft natural studio light, very subtle paper texture, front-facing clean composition, generous negative space, static camera. The bars represent contribution proportions, not a stock-price chart. No letters, words, numbers, financial symbols, coins, people, extra bars, gradients, glows, or additional marks. End on a quiet stable frame suitable for overlaying an original vector logo. Duration 4 seconds, landscape 16:9.

## Production rules

- Keep Example mode identifiable. The delivered film uses UI screenshots and explanatory graphics. Any future Live recording must preserve its actual observation time and show only the real response captured during recording.
- No private wallet or account data in frames. Any public demonstration address must be explicitly labeled as such.
- Captions should remain inside a 90 px horizontal and 70 px vertical safe area at 1080p. Use a maximum of two short lines, forest or charcoal on an opaque warm background.
- Use narration and original or properly licensed audio only. Silence or a quiet original bed is preferable to an unlicensed music track.
- Do not label a video file “Higgsfield-generated” unless the Higgsfield job actually completed. Record model, job ID, duration, and generated portion in the media delivery note.
- Use the final deployed URL only after it resolves and the demo has been checked. Until then, end on “Make a plan” and the product name without an invented domain.
