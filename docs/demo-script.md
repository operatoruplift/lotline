# Lotline: 90-second demo

**0–15 seconds — The problem.**
“A clear plan for your next xStocks contribution. You choose the assets and proportions; Lotline shows exactly how your next USDC contribution splits, and approximately how many units you would receive.” Start at the homepage and choose Make a plan.

**15–35 seconds — Make a split.**
Select three issuer-verified xStocks, enter 50%, 30%, and 20%, and enter 1,000 USDC. Point to the allocation bar and exact amounts. Explain that these percentages apply to this contribution, not the existing portfolio. If services are unavailable, switch visibly to Example mode and say that its balances and estimates are deterministic fixtures.

**35–55 seconds — Read balances and request an estimate.**
In Live mode, paste a public address you are comfortable showing and load balances. No extension or signature is needed. Choose Get estimates and show the quote timestamp. If a live service fails, show its honest unavailable state and continue the walk-through in Example mode. Never present example data as live.

**55–70 seconds — Change the contribution.**
Change the budget to 500 USDC. Observe that the old results clear. Request new estimates. Explain the three columns: current units, estimated contribution units, estimated resulting units. Existing raw units are added to quoted raw output before the current Token-2022 scaling conversion.

**70–85 seconds — Take the plan with you.**
Copy the plan and download the CSV. Copy one output mint and its exact USDC allocation. Open Jupiter, select that mint, enter the copied USDC amount, and review the current amounts and fees there. Do not make a trade during this demo. Lotline does not observe or record a completed purchase.

**85–90 seconds — Why Solana.**
“Real wallet holdings and real tokenized-stock liquidity on Solana, brought together in one clear contribution plan.”
